## What we collect

When you send a question through the contact form:

- your name, and your email address or phone number (or both);
- what your question is about, the programme you ask about, and your message;
- the internet address and browser details your device sent with the form, which we keep to stop misuse of the form.

When you apply for a programme (when online applications open):

- your name, date of birth, contact details, identity document number, nationality and county;
- your education history and the documents you upload, such as certificates and identity documents;
- for applicants under 18, the details of a parent or guardian and their consent;
- the programme and intake you choose, and the progress of your application.

When you study with us, we also keep student records such as registration, timetables, results and fees. Staff who use the staff portal have an account with their name, email address and role.

We do not use this website to collect data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data.

## How we protect it

The website uses encrypted connections. Passwords are stored only in a protected, one-way form. Staff can see only the data their role needs, and actions on records are logged. Uploaded documents are private and reachable only by authorised staff through short-lived links.

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- be told how your data is used, which is the purpose of this notice;
- see the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
- ask us to delete data we no longer have a reason to keep;
- object to our use of your data in some cases;
- withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting what was done before;
- receive your data in a format you can take elsewhere, where the Act allows.
