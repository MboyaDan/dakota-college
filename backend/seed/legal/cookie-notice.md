This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies this website uses.

## Public pages

Our public pages, such as programmes, admissions and contact, do not set cookies and do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

If you follow a link to another service, such as WhatsApp or a map, that service's own cookie and privacy rules apply.

## Signing in

When staff (and later, students) sign in to a portal, we set one cookie:

- Name: dtc_session (shown as __Host-dtc_session on the live site)
- Purpose: keeps you signed in and protects your account
- Type: strictly necessary; the portal does not work without it
- How long: until you sign out, or at most 12 hours, and it stops working after 30 minutes without activity

This cookie cannot be read by scripts on the page and is only sent to this website.

Because it is strictly necessary for a service you ask for, we do not ask for consent to it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but you will not be able to sign in.

## Changes

If we add any other cookie, for example to measure how the site is used, we will update this notice first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
