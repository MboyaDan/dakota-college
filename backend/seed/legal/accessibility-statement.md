Dakota Training College wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, zoom, or a slow phone connection.

## Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every public page is checked with automated accessibility tests before each release, on a small phone screen and on a desktop screen.
- Pages work with a keyboard, show where the focus is, and have a "Skip to main content" link.
- Text and buttons meet colour contrast requirements, and colour is never the only way information is shown.
- Forms have labels above each field and error messages that say what to fix.
- Pages respect the setting to reduce motion.
- The site is designed for phones first and keeps pages light for mobile data.
